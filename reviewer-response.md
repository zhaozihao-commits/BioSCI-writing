# SCI point-by-point reviewer response and editor cover-letter standards

## Priorities

Be respectful, precise, concise, and transparent. Reviewers should quickly see (1) what was asked, (2) what was done, (3) where to find it, (4) what remains unresolved.

- Keep each reviewer's original comment intact when reproducing it; do not silently rewrite reviewer English.
- Do not turn impossible/omitted experiments into claims of completion. Cite actual revisions rather than saying `all concerns were fully resolved`.
- Use passive voice naturally and avoid a succession of sentences starting `We...`.
- For simple comments on the H1.5 paper, user's preferred opening is `Thanks for your valuable comment.`
- For the COL6A5 ABBS minor-revision response, preferred opening is `Thank you for this important and constructive comment.` or `Thank you for this constructive comment.`
- `According to the reviewer’s suggestion, ...` is acceptable but avoid repeating it mechanically for every paragraph.

## Default response structure

1. One-sentence thanks; briefly acknowledge a real issue if relevant.
2. State concrete action: manuscript wording, graphical abstract, figure legends, extra data, expanded Methods, revised discussion.
3. Where needed, explain scientific evidence and remaining limits without overdefending.
4. Give **verified** section + page/line or figure/table reference using final manuscript pagination.

Example (template; adapt truthfully):

`Response: Thank you for this constructive comment. The Results section has been revised to distinguish the predominant promoter responses from the limited effects of the other predicted sites (Fig. 6D and 6G; Page XX). Because reporter assays reflect promoter activity rather than direct transcription-factor occupancy, the interpretation has been made more cautious.`

## Revision types

**Biochemical binding not verified:** Say plainly whether Co-IP / GST pull-down was performed. Explain genuine difficulty (large multidomain ECM protein; membrane receptor), distinguish full-length and domain-based approaches, and say where the limits were acknowledged. Do not present computational interaction predictions as validated physical binding. Do not claim a domain assay is impossible; say the design/expression/purification/optimization would need additional work.

**Small hospital cohort:** Explicitly distinguish the `n = 3` in-house IHC/WB cohort from TCGA or other public dataset statistics. State representativeness/generalizability limits; propose validation in larger independent cohorts.

**Second CArG-box site:** Briefly summarize site-specific luciferase and double-mutation evidence; main effect of COL6A5 P1 and ANTXR1 P2, limited effect from the others; do not say those other sites are universally without function. Locate exactly in Results.

**Editor: at least two siRNAs:** Screening three candidates and using just the most effective siRNA downstream **does not satisfy** two independent siRNAs for functional validation. If not completed, state the limitation and reason transparently. Complementary rescue or stable shRNA approaches strengthen but do not substitute for this specific requirement.

**Editor: Methods / English:** Specify which subsections were reorganized and what real details added (WB, IHC, MTT, HUVEC CM/tube, luciferase, ChIP, animals) rather than generic claims. Only assert changes actually present.

## Cover letter and formatting

- Journal title and manuscript ID exact; identify second-round revision where applicable.
- Cover letter is concise: gratitude → major revision categories → how comments were answered → request for consideration. Avoid overly confident `all issues conclusively addressed`.
- Reviewer responses in a separate structured section; no duplicate cover-letter paragraphs after signature.
- Gray vs grey: the user's chosen manuscript British style uses `grey`; use consistently when describing revision highlighting.
- Locations can change after layout; verify against the actual final rendered Word/PDF, not from earlier versions or memory.
- Reference lists embedded in the rebuttal may be numbered independently from main manuscript references: verify each independently and match DOI metadata.
