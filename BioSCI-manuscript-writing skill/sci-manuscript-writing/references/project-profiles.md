# Project-specific routing: never generalize specific experimental data

Use these profiles solely to choose vocabulary, scientific cautions, and information-placement conventions. **Do not treat listed examples as evidence of performed experiments in a new manuscript.** Consult the latest user file for the current experiment.

## TPH@Pd-TTF metal-organic cage / photothermal nanorobot

- Scientific framing: TPH host/acceptor, Pd nodes with POD-like activity, TTF guest/donor, donor–acceptor (D–A) electronic coupling; NIR photothermal therapy, ROS/POD-like catalysis, possible GSH depletion, thermophoresis-associated active transport, tumor spheroid penetration.
- Claim caution: distinguish materials characterization from proof of cage topology/guest encapsulation. NMR chemical shifts, broadened signals, UV-Vis-NIR absorption, NIR heating, nanozyme catalysis and cell uptake each support different questions; do not equate one with another.
- M&M style established by user: concise, objective, natural operating order, central listing of materials, supplier details and instruments in `Materials and Instrumentation`; downstream methods retain only essential conditions/readouts. Avoid `Briefly` and `To evaluate` starts.
- Use user-approved treatment conditions only if the actual experiment confirms them (e.g. 808 nm, power density, duration, spin labels, 3D spheroid depth). Do not borrow historical values blindly.
- Use clear nanomedicine vocabulary: `D–A interaction`, `POD-like activity`, `thermophoretic nanomotors`, `photothermal conversion`, `GSH depletion`, `penetration into tumor spheroids`. Do not overstate weak PDT/ROS pathways.

## COL6A5 / ANTXR1 / RhoA / MRTF-A CRC mechanism paper (ABBS)

- Distinguish correlation, genetic rescue, binding, and mechanism. ANTXR1 can be functionally associated with COL6A5 without proven direct ligand–receptor binding.
- Hypothesis: functional signaling through RhoA activation and MRTF-A regulation, possibly reinforced by MRTF-A-responsive COL6A5 and ANTXR1 promoters. If feedback is not closed by direct binding, use a potential regulatory mechanism.
- Differentiate `COL6A5 P1` and `ANTXR1 P2` as predominant reporter-responsive elements from secondary putative sites whose effects appear limited only under tested conditions.
- Distinguish HCT116 conditioned medium→HUVEC tube formation from direct COL6A5 knockdown in HUVECs.
- In-house clinical specimens n = 3 must not be conflated with TCGA survival analyses.
- Tail-vein injection reflects experimental pulmonary colonization. Nude-mouse immunodeficiency constrains immunological extrapolation.
- ABBS office asked for at least two independently validated siRNAs; three screened and one used downstream is incomplete compliance.
- This manuscript uses method subsections with real supplier/catalog details when necessary for reproducibility. Tables and figures can change numbering; inspect final version.

## SCFA / histone H1.5 / sodium propionate (NaP) CRC revision

- Main themes: NaP-induced histone H1.5 propionylation; p300 and SIRT1 regulation; differentiation and apoptosis in HT29/HCT116; H1.5K27R mutation.
- Distinguish mutation effects from site-specific modification evidence: K27R can alter protein structure/binding/stability and alone does not prove K27 propionylation is indispensable.
- Avoid `indispensable prerequisite`; prefer qualified functional contribution where direct site-specific antibody or biochemical proof lacks.
- Acknowledge lack of independent evaluation of NaP-associated medium pH, Na⁺, osmolarity when discussing concentrations.
- Reviewer-response preferred introductory phrase for routine comments: `Thanks for your valuable comment.` Keep short, passive where natural, figures and table locations precise, no speculative new experiment claims.
