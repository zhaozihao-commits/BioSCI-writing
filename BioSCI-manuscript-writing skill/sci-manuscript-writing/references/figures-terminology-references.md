# Figures, nomenclature, abbreviation, and reference standards

## Figure legends and graphics

- Describe each panel by letter, actual experiment, treatment/control, measured outcome, relevant scale bar, sample size, summary statistic, and applied test where needed. Do not paste a mini Discussion into legends.
- `Fig. 1A`, `Fig. S1A`, `Table S1`, `Table S2` etc. must be consistent in body, response, figures, and supplement. Verify Fig. 6D/6G promoter mutations, Fig. 7 mouse growth, Fig. 8 lung colonization separately.
- Report comparison groups for stars and hash symbols as per actual underlying statistics.
- No unsupported claim of image integrity, novelty, or duplication screening without inspecting the actual figures/records.
- Graphical abstracts distinguish proposed relationships with question marks/dashed arrows and cautious labels (`Proposed feedback`); avoid broken word-wrap (`feedbac / k`) or direct solid ligand–receptor arrows where binding is unproven.

## Nomenclature

Human gene symbols are generally uppercase italic, e.g. *COL6A5*, *ANTXR1*, *MRTFA*, *ARHGEF2*, *ARHGAP35*. Corresponding protein names are upright, e.g. COL6A5, ANTXR1, MRTF-A. For mouse genes use italic initial upper/mixed lower e.g. *Col6a5*, *Antxr1*, *Mrtfa*, *Pcna*. Determine whether tissue qPCR assayed human xenograft transcripts or mouse host transcript based on primers, **not merely the capitalization in manuscript**.

- `knockdown` != `knockout` / `depletion` / `deletion` unless independently supported.
- `CArG-box` for motifs, `P1` and `P2` exact locations as shown in figures.
- `POD-like activity`, `GSH depletion`, `thermophoretic motion`, `photothermal therapy (PTT)`, `photodynamic therapy (PDT)` for applicable nanomedicine writing.
- TPH@Pd and TPH@Pd-TTF strings must remain uniform across figures and prose, with the user's current approved chemical name spelling used consistently.
- Upon first meaningful mention define abbreviations as the journal requires. Do not redefine the same acronym mechanically in every Methods subsection.

## Numbers and formatting

- `37 °C`, `24 h`, `30 min`, `1 × 10⁵ cells`, `n = 3`, `mean ± SD`, `4 μm`, `1 W cm⁻²`, `100 μg mL⁻¹`.
- British vs American spelling should be consistent with the selected journal/manuscript; `grey` for second-round ABBS gray-highlight notation.
- Use hyphens for compounds modifying nouns (`COL6A5-knockdown cells`), but `COL6A5 knockdown suppressed ...` as a noun phrase.
- Avoid malformed grammar (`First, Nevertheless`, duplicate `Second`, `experimental experimental`, `may be` vs `maybe`, `using a reader`).

## References

- Verify from actual DOI/publisher/PubMed when accessible, never invent or autofill author/title/journal/pages/article number from a partial match.
- Independently check **whether each reference supports the cited claim**. Related collagen subtypes are not interchangeable proof of a COL6A5 or COL6 receptor hypothesis. A membrane detergent methods paper does not prove COL6A5–ANTXR1 interaction.
- Verify final journal issue year vs online-first date: choose the correct volume-year-page/article combination.
- Maintain exact numbering after adding citations. Find uncited reference entries and body citations that have no bibliography entry.
- The user's DOI-to-reference formatting sample follows BBA style when requested:
  `[n] I. Surname, I. Surname, Article title, Abbreviated Journal Title volume (year) pages/article number. https://doi.org/DOI.`
  For an ABBS paper use the journal's mandated style, not the BBA default.
